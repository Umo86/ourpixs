## 3. Website content and pages missing

What converting competitors show that we should add:

1. Live demo album plus demo QR (Lense and Kululu both offer try-it demos; POV has a demo camera). Biggest single conversion lever for a no-app product.
2. How-it-works section with 3 steps and a 30-60 second video (Wedibox has a dedicated how-it-works page).
3. Compare pages: "BizimResimler vs Google Photos", "vs WhatsApp groups", "vs Wedibox / GuestPix / Kululu" (Wedibox, GuestCam, Wedding Studio, Fotify, Knipsmig all run these and rank for competitor names).
4. Money-back guarantee badge on pricing and checkout (six competitors have one).
5. Pricing page with "what is included" comparison table, FAQ under it, and "pay once, no subscription" line (most competitors lead with this).
6. Use-case landing pages per occasion: wedding, birthday, henna/kına, sünnet, engagement/nişan, baby shower, graduation, corporate, house party (Wedibox has /occasions/weddings; Kululu and Knipsmig similar). In Turkish: düğün, kına gecesi, nişan, sünnet, doğum günü.
7. Blog / SEO guides: "how to collect wedding photos from guests", "QR code for wedding photos", "Google Photos vs QR album", and Turkish equivalents "düğün fotoğraflarını misafirlerden nasıl toplarım", "kına gecesi QR kod". Competitors (Wedibox, Knipsmig, Fotify, GuestCam) all publish best-of listicles.
8. Sample QR sign / table card gallery with free downloads (Canva template links plus PDF), which also serves as lead magnet.
9. Testimonials / reviews: we have none. Plan: seed with beta events, ask first 20 hosts for a quote and photo permission; show a count only once it is real. Kululu leads with "4.7 from 500+ reviews". Do not invent reviews.
10. Trust and privacy page: where data is stored, UK GDPR, private by default, who can see, deletion on request, full-quality originals. Add accessibility statement and cookie banner (UK PECR/GDPR).
11. Help centre / FAQ topics: do guests need an app, what if no signal at the venue (POV flags this), photo limits, can guests see each other's photos, how long is storage, what if I miss the download, can I delete a photo, how to print the QR, how to use on a projector, refunds. Include "what if the venue has no Wi-Fi".
12. Venue / DJ / photographer partner page with commission or free QR cards (Partner £19/mo needs its own landing page, a sample co-branded album, and an affiliate or referral code; Wedibox accepts partner codes at checkout).
13. Gift cards (nobody major seems to push this; low priority but good for wedding gifts and for Turkish gift culture).
14. Press / "as seen in" logos: only add when real. Until then show "built in London, for UK and Turkish families" rather than fake logos.
15. Contact / support: visible email or WhatsApp chat widget. WhatsApp support suits Turkish users.
16. Turkish-specific page set at bizimresimler.com (not just a translation): price in £ and a TL equivalent note, local payment methods if Stripe supports them, and diaspora angle (Turkish families in UK/Germany/Netherlands).

## 4. Pricing insights

- Market anchor: paid wedding albums cluster at $29-$49 entry, $79-$99 top for one-time payment per event; UK-specific "from" prices are £22 (QRpict), £29 (VowLens), £37-39 (GuestCam, GuestPix). Disposable-camera apps price by guest count ($4.99-$99.99).
- Our One Day £19 is the cheapest of the serious options except QRpict (£22 flat, but with 1-year storage) and Lense (£4.99 small events). It reads as a party/birthday price and fits henna and sünnet. Check that One Day storage is not too short; competitors give 3-12 months.
- Our Big Day £39 with 5 days and 3-year storage is roughly in line with Wedibox Wedding ($49, 6 months) and beats everyone on storage length (only Knipsmig offers lifetime). Lead with "3 years of storage" and "multi-day". Probably underpriced vs value: if conversion is healthy, test £49.
- Free tier: 1 day, 100 photos, 30-day storage is more generous on photos than Wedibox/GuestPix/Fotify (50 each) and equal to Knipsmig on retention. Risk: Knipsmig's free tier is unlimited photos, so do not market free as the headline limit; sell it as "try it at a small party".
- Partner £19/month for venues/DJs/photographers: the market has almost no published partner pricing (Wedding Arrivals, GuestPix vendors, POV business). Needs clear included quota (events/month, branding, co-hosting). Test annual discount.
- Common add-ons: video (Lense +$9.99), unlimited captures, face search (+$45), photo booth ($49), phone guestbook (+$30), printed QR cards or sign, photobooks (VowLens top tier, POV, Guestlense $199), lifetime storage, guest ZIP downloads ($9.99 each on Knipsmig free).
- Mistakes to avoid:
  1. Pricing by GB (Anılar Bulutta) punishes video and is confusing.
  2. Short upload windows (3 months at entry on Wedibox, GuestPix, VowLens): destination and multi-day weddings need longer. Define whether our window starts at event date (Wedibox does) rather than purchase date.
  3. Hiding price details or showing fake "list" prices with permanent 50% off (Kululu, GuestCam use this; reads as gimmicky).
  4. Mixing currencies on one page (CatchMemo listing). Show £ for UK, TL/EUR option for Turkish site.
  5. Paywalling the ZIP download (Knipsmig free tier) creates resentment; keep download of everything on every plan, even free during the 30 days.
  6. Gating video and voice behind the middle tier only (VowLens). Our features are all in; keep it simple.
  7. Do not add RSVP/website just to match Wedibox; it dilutes the product and invites support load.
  8. Refund terms: keep simple, say what counts as "used" (Knipsmig voids if you downloaded photos).
