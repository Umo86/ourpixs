The demo account already has a sample event ("Elif & Can's Wedding") with photos, challenges and guestbook messages.
To try the guest side, open the event > QR tab and enter the event code shown under the QR on the Join screen (Welcome > Join an event by QR > Event code).
Guests can delete their own uploads; hosts can hide or delete any upload (long-press in the album).
The app contains no purchases, no prices and no links to external purchasing. It only shows the status of each event's gallery (for example "Live until 14 June, 22:00" or "Setup"). The demo event's gallery is active (set up by our team), so every feature, including uploads and downloads, is available.
