Our Pixs collects every guest's photos and videos from your wedding, engagement, henna night, birthday or party into one private album. Guests simply scan your QR code. No app for them, no sign-up, no passwords.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Create your event in a minute: pick the occasion, add names, date and venue.
2. Print or share your QR code. Put it on tables, invitations or the big screen.
3. Guests scan, type their name and start uploading straight from their phone's browser.
4. Every shot lands in your album, in full quality, sorted by day and moment.

MADE FOR HOSTS
- One album for everyone: photos and videos from every guest, in one place.
- Full-quality originals, saved to your camera roll in a tap.
- Approve before showing: keep the album tidy by approving uploads first.
- Hide or delete anything with a long-press. Favourite the best shots.
- Guest PIN to keep your album private.
- Next-morning reveal: keep the album a surprise until the day after.

FUN FOR GUESTS
- Photo challenges like "Selfie with the couple" or "Best dance move".
- Disposable camera mode: a limited number of shots, just like film.
- Guestbook: leave a note for the hosts.
- Live wall: a slideshow of new photos on a TV or projector as they arrive.

MULTI-DAY CELEBRATIONS
Henna night, wedding and after party in one event, with photos tagged by day.

FOR FAMILY FAR AWAY
Share a read-only link so relatives abroad can enjoy the photos too.

ENGLISH AND TURKISH
The app and guest pages work in English and Turkish, and follow each guest's phone language.

FREE TRIAL
Your 3-day free trial starts when you create your first event, so you can set everything up and invite your guests. Downloads are not included in the trial. Hosting after the trial is a subscription that covers everything, with no limits on events, photos or videos.

PRIVACY
Your album is private to your event. Guests agree before they upload, can delete their own uploads, and you can remove anything at any time. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.
